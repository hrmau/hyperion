# Grid Gorilla name and logo

The code in this repository is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0 (`LICENSE`), and Grid Gorilla's datasets and written analysis under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (`LICENSE-DATA`). **Neither licence grants any
right to use the Grid Gorilla name, the gorilla logo, or the gridgorilla.app
domain to identify your own project.** Those identify one site, and keeping them
distinct is what stops a copy being mistaken for it.

## What is fine

- Saying that your project is based on, forked from, or uses data from Grid
  Gorilla, and linking to gridgorilla.app or this repository.
- Referring to Grid Gorilla by name when writing or talking about it.
- Running an unmodified copy locally to develop against or contribute back.
- Screenshots of gridgorilla.app, with attribution.

## What needs a rename first

If you deploy a copy, modified or not, where other people can reach it:

- Give it a different name, and do not use "Grid Gorilla" or a confusingly
  similar name for it.
- Replace the logo and everything derived from it: `public/gorilla*.svg`,
  `public/gridgorilla_logo*.svg`, `public/icons/`, `public/og.png`,
  `public/og_country.png` and `docs/design-system/assets/`.
- Do not present it in a way that suggests it is gridgorilla.app or endorsed by
  it.

The AGPL still applies to such a copy in full, including making your modified
source available to its users.
