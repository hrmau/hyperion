`HookCard` is a claim you can click. Three across, one green.

```jsx
<HookCard tone="accent" eyebrow="Higher or lower" title="Does Ireland or Spain get more of its power from wind?" footnote="58% of Irish visitors get this wrong." />
<HookCard tone="card" eyebrow="Your neighbours" title="The UK cut coal to zero. Ireland still has 6%.">Moneypoint is the last one standing.</HookCard>
<HookCard tone="strong" eyebrow="The world" title="A third of the planet still runs on coal.">Zoom out to all 214 grids.</HookCard>
```

Titles are claims, not nouns — "The UK cut coal to zero", never "Coal comparison". Stack to one column below 768px, keeping the accent card first.
