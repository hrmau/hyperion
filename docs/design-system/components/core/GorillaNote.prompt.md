`GorillaNote` is the voice of the product: what the chart above actually means, in one sentence.

```jsx
<GorillaNote lead="Watch the green band.">
  In 1990 it didn't exist. Ireland went from zero to second in the world inside one
  working career — and gas is what covers the days the wind doesn't blow.
</GorillaNote>
```

Rules: one per chart, 2 sentences maximum, second person, no exclamation marks, no emoji. It should say something a reader could not get from the axis labels.
