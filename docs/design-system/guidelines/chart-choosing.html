THE QUESTIONTHE FORM
What is this grid made of, over time?StackedAreaChart — the signature form
What is it made of right now?MixBar, or DonutStat for a single share
How much of X, over time?LineChart — up to four series, then small multiples
Who is top / bottom?RankedBarList, or DataTable past ~15 rows
These two grids, head to head?DiffBar, or mirrored StackedAreaChart
Where in the world?WorldMap — but only when geography is the point
Every grid, equal weight?TileGrid — a map hides Singapore and Iceland
Does X relate to Y?ScatterChart, log X, with a causation caveat
Direction, inside a row?Sparkline
EVERY CHART GETS ONE GorillaNote UNDER IT, AND EVERY CHART IN PROSE GETS A SOURCE LINE.
